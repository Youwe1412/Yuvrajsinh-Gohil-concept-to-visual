## g. Negative results and what they changed

- **The guard blocked a day's work and was removed.** 0.7.0 built it; 0.9.0 recorded the P2 friction; 1.0.0 made AEM a notary ([ABOUT.md §12](ABOUT.md), commit `705c5b3`). P2's `events.jsonl` holds 5 denial events from 6 Oct.
- **The "3 of 13 additions" claim was wrong.** The brief expected 45 asked and 13 added; the real placements give 50 and 8, and the 3 changes sat inside asked scenes ([ABOUT.md §13.2](ABOUT.md)). `aem calibrate` still prints "3 of 58 additions" for P1 (no plan column): development data, not evidence.
- **P1 numbers are retrospective.** AEM did not exist during P1.
- **The hand-made baseline does not exist.** The three baseline moments were built by the pipeline at the operator's request (P2 `inputs.md`); the control is gone.
- **`aem moments` over-counts.** 64 rows for 42 moments; 22 are table headings parsed from the plan ([P2-STATUS.md](P2-STATUS.md)). `aem calibrate` says 53 delivered. Both defects.
- **Renders not on disk at snapshot.** P2's `renders/` and evidence folders are empty at 23:50; every check's record stands in `events.jsonl`.
